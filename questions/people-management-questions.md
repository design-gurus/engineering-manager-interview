# People Management Interview Questions

These 20 questions cover the seven subjects that people management rounds return to: low performers, firing, hiring, delegation, top performers, conflict, and diversity. Each group opens with the signal the interviewer is measuring. Prepare one real case per group, and you will be able to answer every question in it.

## Low performers

**Measuring:** how early you act, and how clearly you define improvement.

1. Tell me about a time you managed someone who was not meeting expectations.
2. How do you tell the difference between a skill gap and a motivation gap?
3. Describe a performance improvement plan you wrote. What were its goals?
4. Tell me about a low performer who recovered. What made the difference?

A performance improvement plan is a written agreement that lists specific outcomes and a deadline, usually 30 to 90 days.

## Firing

**Measuring:** whether you can make a hard decision fairly and take responsibility for it.

5. Have you ever let someone go? Describe the process from the first concern to the last day.
6. How did you know that further coaching would not work?
7. How did you explain the departure to the rest of the team?

## Hiring

**Measuring:** whether you hire on evidence, and whether you question the need before opening a role.

8. How do you decide that your team needs another person?
9. Describe your interview process. What does each step measure?
10. Tell me about a hire that did not work out. What did you miss?
11. How do you persuade a candidate who has two other offers to join your team?

## Delegation

**Measuring:** whether you match work to people deliberately and stay accountable for the result.

12. How do you decide which tasks to delegate and which to keep?
13. Tell me about a time you delegated something and it went badly.
14. How do you delegate to someone more senior or more technical than you?

## Top performers

**Measuring:** whether you grow your strongest people or only depend on them.

15. How do you keep your best engineer motivated?
16. Tell me about someone you helped get promoted. What did you do specifically?
17. Your strongest engineer wants to move to another team. What do you do?

## Conflict

**Measuring:** whether you reach a decision without choosing a favorite or avoiding the issue.

18. Tell me about a conflict between two people on your team and how it ended.
19. Describe a disagreement between your team and another team. What was your part in resolving it?

## Diversity and inclusion

**Measuring:** whether you have taken a concrete action, with a result, and not only stated a belief.

20. What have you done to build a more diverse team, and what was the outcome?

## A quick test for any answer

Before the interview, check each prepared answer against four questions.

| Check | A passing answer |
|---|---|
| Is it one case? | It names one person's situation, not a general policy |
| Is there a date? | It says how many days or weeks passed before you acted |
| Is there a number? | It gives a measure before and after |
| Is there a lesson? | It ends with one thing you now do differently |

**Example of a passing opening line:** "A senior engineer on my team of eight missed three review deadlines in five weeks, and I raised it in our next one-on-one."
