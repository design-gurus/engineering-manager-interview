# A Four-Week Preparation Plan

This plan prepares a working manager for an engineering manager loop in four weeks, at about six hours a week. Each week has one goal and a condition that tells you it is finished. A first-time manager candidate should add two weeks: one before week 1 for collecting evidence, and one after week 2 for extra story practice.

## The plan at a glance

| Week | Goal | Hours | Finished when |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Understand the loop and build the story bank | 6 | Ten story cards are written |
| 2 | People management and behavioral answers | 6 | Twelve answers are recorded and reviewed |
| 3 | Project retrospective and system design | 7 | One 20-minute presentation and three designs are done |
| 4 | Situational questions, coding, and full practice | 6 | Two mock interviews are completed |

A mock interview is a practice interview with another person who plays the interviewer.

## Week 1: The loop and the story bank

- Read [The interview loop](../rounds/interview-loop.md) and ask the recruiter for the exact list of rounds. (30 minutes)
- Read [Engineer and manager roles compared](ic-vs-manager.md) if this is your first manager role. (20 minutes)
- Write ten story cards with the [story bank](story-bank.md), and collect the numbers for each. (4 hours)
- Fill in the mapping grid and find the columns with fewer than two marks. (30 minutes)
- Read [STAR for managers](star-for-managers.md). (30 minutes)

## Week 2: People management and behavioral

- Read [People management](../rounds/people-management.md) and [Behavioral](../rounds/behavioral.md). (40 minutes)
- Record answers to six [people management questions](../questions/people-management-questions.md), one per subject. (2 hours)
- Record answers to six [behavioral questions](../questions/behavioral-questions.md), one per family. (2 hours)
- Review every recording against [Red flags](red-flags.md) and re-record the three weakest. (80 minutes)

## Week 3: Project retrospective and system design

- Choose the project with the scoring table in [Project retrospective](../rounds/project-retrospective.md). (30 minutes)
- Write the presentation outline and present it aloud twice with a 20-minute timer. (2 hours)
- Prepare answers to ten follow-up questions about that project. (1 hour)
- Read [System design for managers](../rounds/system-design-for-managers.md). (30 minutes)
- Practice three design questions at 45 minutes each, and end each with a delivery plan. (3 hours)

## Week 4: Situational, coding, and full practice

- Read [Situational](../rounds/situational.md) and answer eight [situational questions](../questions/situational-questions.md) aloud. (90 minutes)
- If the loop has a coding round, solve ten problems while explaining aloud. If it does not, use the time for two more design questions. (2 hours)
- Complete two mock interviews: one on people management and one on the project retrospective. (2 hours)
- Prepare three [questions to ask](questions-to-ask-the-interviewer.md) for each interviewer. (30 minutes)

## If you have less time

| Time available | Keep | Drop |
|---|---|---|
| Two weeks | Story bank, people management answers, project presentation, one mock interview | Coding practice beyond five problems, the third design question |
| One week | Six story cards, six recorded answers, the project presentation | Mock interviews, coding |
| Two days | Three stories (low performer, late project, conflict) and the project outline | Everything else |

## The day before

- Reread the ten story cards once, without rewriting them.
- Assign each story to a round, so that none is used twice.
- Confirm the schedule, the names of the interviewers, and the coding language.
